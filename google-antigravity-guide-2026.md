# Google Antigravity 2026: Sign Up, Install & Account Safety Guide
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>AI coding tools are moving beyond code completion to handling entire development tasks. Developers can describe a goal to an AI agent, which can inspect a project, edit files, run tests, and open a browser to check the result. Google Antigravity has drawn attention among developers because it brings those steps into a single workflow.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For first-time users, the biggest obstacles may be installation and sign-in. Should you download the Antigravity App or Antigravity IDE? Can you use an existing Gmail account? Why might the browser show successful authorization while the desktop client remains signed out? This guide covers account preparation, downloading and installing Antigravity, getting started, and protecting your account to help you use the tool more reliably.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>I. What Is Google Antigravity?</strong><strong></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://antigravity.google/"><u>Google Antigravity</u></a>&nbsp;is an AI agent development platform from Google. Developers can describe tasks in natural language and let an agent read project files, modify code, run terminal commands, and inspect websites through a browser. It also supports managing multiple agents and scheduling tasks, helping teams organize development, testing, and repetitive work.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the launch of Antigravity 2.0, its product lineup has expanded to include a standalone desktop app, Antigravity IDE, a command-line interface (CLI), IDE extensions, and an SDK for different development workflows.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>II. Preparing Your Google Antigravity Account</strong><strong></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You do not need to register a separate Antigravity account. Eligible users can sign in with their Google account. Before downloading and installing the software, however, it helps to check your account eligibility and network connection.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. Google Account Requirements</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Google Antigravity supports personal Google accounts, including free accounts and accounts subscribed to Google AI Pro or Google AI Ultra. If you do not have one yet, see our <a href="https://www.ipfoxy.com/blog/ideas-inspiration/7654"><u>guide to creating a Gmail account</u></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For most individual developers, an existing personal Gmail account is enough; you do not have to purchase a subscription before getting started. Access for business or school Google Workspace accounts depends on Google’s enterprise onboarding requirements.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2. Age Requirement</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Google Antigravity requires users to be at least 18 years old.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you see an “Age Unverified” message during sign-in, open your <a href="https://myaccount.google.com/personal-info"><u>Google Account settings</u></a>, check the date of birth on your profile, and follow Google’s instructions to verify your age.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>3. Supported Countries and Regions</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Google lists countries and regions where Antigravity is available, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Japan, and Singapore. Eligibility can also depend on the country or region associated with your Google account.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Even if you are physically in a supported country, you may see an unavailable-region or ineligible-account warning if your associated account region does not qualify. Check your current associated country and, if the information is incorrect, submit an official request to correct it. See <a href="https://www.ipfoxy.com/blog/ideas-inspiration/7880"><u>How to Change Your Google Account Region</u></a></p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>III. How to Download and Install Google Antigravity: Step-by-Step</strong><strong></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Google Antigravity supports Windows, macOS, and Linux. The walkthrough below uses Windows to explain downloading the desktop app, installing it, and signing in.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. Download the Desktop App</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open the <a href="https://antigravity.google/download"><u>official Google Antigravity download page</u></a>&nbsp;and find Antigravity App. On Windows, go to Settings &gt; System &gt; About to check your system type, then choose the x64 or ARM64 installer. Google currently lists Windows 10 (64-bit) or later as the minimum requirement. On macOS, choose Apple Silicon or Intel; on Linux, select the appropriate architecture and check the listed dependencies.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>V. Google Antigravity Account Safety and Suspension Prevention</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As Antigravity becomes more popular, some developers are trying to access its models through third-party tools, while others have reported temporary restrictions on their accounts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To use Antigravity consistently over time, pay attention not only to connectivity but also to how models are accessed and to Google’s official service policies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. Use Authorized Model Access, Not Unapproved Third-Party Proxies</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Some developers want to use Antigravity models through third-party coding tools such as OpenCode or Claude Code to reduce context switching or model costs. Google warns that accessing Antigravity through unauthorized third-party software, tools, or services may violate its terms and result in suspension or termination.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the official Antigravity app, CLI, or supported IDE extensions where possible instead of routing model requests through unauthorized third-party services.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you need Gemini models in a third-party coding tool, use an official API key or a Google-supported enterprise integration.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2. Maintain a Stable Network Connection</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Model calls, agent execution, and browser automation depend on reliable connectivity. Repeated disconnections, a failed proxy connection, or excessive latency can disrupt tasks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use a connection you have already tested and avoid frequent changes during authorization or active sessions. If problems occur, check the exit IP, connectivity, and latency before relying on poorly maintained public proxy servers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Developers who require a stable, fixed proxy connection can use IPFoxy proxies to maintain a consistent exit IP and check connection quality with <a href="https://tools.ipfoxy.com/tools-proxycheck/"><u>proxy testing tools</u></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>VI. Google Antigravity FAQs</strong><strong></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>1. Is Google Antigravity free? Eligible personal Google accounts can access a free tier. Google AI Pro and Ultra subscriptions affect which models and usage limits are available. Try a small project on the free tier before deciding whether to upgrade.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>2. What should I do if my Google Antigravity account is suspended?<br>First determine whether only Antigravity access is restricted or your entire Google account is disabled. For Antigravity restrictions, follow the notice or <a href="https://antigravity.google/docs/faq"><u>official Antigravity FAQ</u></a>&nbsp;for support. If your Google account is disabled, try signing in through a browser and follow the Start Appeal process shown on screen.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>3. What if Google Antigravity says “Not available in your country”? Confirm which Google account was selected during browser authorization, then check its associated country against the Antigravity supported-region list. If the account details are inaccurate, request a correction through Google’s official channels.</strong></p>
