# How to Sign Up for Grok in 2026: Step-by-Step Guide
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Grok is an AI assistant developed by xAI (SpaceX Super Intelligence, or SpaceXSI), with features for chatting, voice interaction, and image and video generation. If you are new to Grok, you may be wondering: Where is it available? How do you sign up? And what do you get with a paid plan?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In this 2026 Grok sign-up guide, we will cover where Grok is available and what you need before registering. Then we will walk you through signing in and sending your first message, before comparing the benefits of Grok Free, X Premium, and Premium+ to help you decide whether to subscribe.</p>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>I</strong><strong>. What Is Grok?</strong><strong></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Grok is an AI assistant from xAI that you can use on its standalone website, in the mobile app, or through X. Beyond chatting, writing, and analyzing files, it can search the web and public posts on X. For example, when researching competitors, you can ask Grok to organize public reactions to a product launch by price, features, and after-sales support, with links back to the original posts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One important update: On October 12, 2026, the company launched <a href="https://x.ai/news/x-subscription?"><u>The X Subscription</u></a>, bringing Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor, and X Premium together under one subscription. New users no longer need to purchase SuperGrok or X Premium separately.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As of November 2026, <a href="https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7"><u>Grok 4.7</u></a>&nbsp;is the company's flagship general-purpose model. It powers Expert mode on the web and Fast mode for paid users, while image, video, and voice features use dedicated models.</p>

The standalone Grok app is also listed in the iOS App Store in markets including the United States, Japan, and Singapore. Features and subscription options on the standalone website and app may vary by location, so check the official Grok website before signing up.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>VI</strong><strong>. Grok Registration FAQs</strong><strong></strong></h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. Can I Sign Up for Grok Without an X Account?</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes. On the standalone website, you can register using the available options, such as email, Google, or Apple. If you later want to access X-related benefits included in your plan, simply connect your personal X account.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2. Do I Need a Phone Number to Sign Up for Grok?</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It depends on your sign-in method and the instructions shown during registration. For example, if you sign in with Google and Google asks you to verify your identity, complete that step on Google first. Then return to Grok and check whether any additional steps are required.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>3. How Can I Keep My Grok Account Secure?</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use the same account across your devices, and keep your subscription and sign-in methods consistent. Where possible, stick to familiar devices and a stable residential IP connection that matches your actual location. You can follow the IPFoxy Chrome proxy setup described above to configure a consistent browser environment, which may help reduce verification prompts and security flags. If your account is compromised, contact official support immediately.</p>
